# Trust Score 0.5 Training Dataset Sampling Strategy

1. Data Quality Filter → Remove impossible/corrupt records

- 
- Remove numbers with clear data ingestion artifacts (duplicate records, timestamp anomalies, null-heavy feature vectors)

2. Lifecycle Completeness Tiering → (A/B/C)

- A. Full lifecycle
- B. Left-censored: Already active when EnStream data begin (missing early behaviours)
    - `observed_lifecycle_fraction` = `days_since_first_observed / estimated_total_active_days`
- C. Fragmented: Very short observation windows, appear and disappear quickly, or have massive gaps
    - *hold these out of the primary training set entirely*

3. Lifecycle Weirdness Scoring → Quarantine outliers

- Define weird quantitatively
    - e.g., total active days, number of activation/deactivation events, longest dormancy gap, behavioral entropy over time, and ratio of active days to calendar span. Then use a simple outlier method (even just percentile thresholds) on these meta-features to flag lifecycle outliers
- *Evaluate separately (might be fraud, might be caused by data quality)*
- *Need to be cross-referenced against the fraud labels*
1. Temporal-based Sampling 
- Undersample or downweight periods near lifecycle boundaries
    - first few days after activation, last few days before deactivation), because behavior during these transitions is inherently atypical and could teach your model that transitional behavior is "normal."
